# Tellers — Grey Man

The Tellers measure what stories are running their civilization. They have existed for thousands and thousands of years.

## Origins

Earth's Tellers are newer. Mindland's are older.

They don't know this about each other — not at first.

## Present State

At present, the Tellers are very far from power in both lands, driven out long before. It is long after Mindland's saving of Earthers.

## The Accidental Meeting

Many Tellers from both places meet accidentally. They realize, over time, the deep similarities between them.

After a long process, they accept that there is some connection — something pointing to the fact that Mindland and human civilization were connected once. Otherwise, how could Tellers of both places be so similar?

That is when their quest for the truth begins.
