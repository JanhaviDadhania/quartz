# Commonplace Book

The Commonplace Book is the name of a gathering — a tradition.

## Origins

It was started thousands of years ago. The custom was to meet in decided common places and write down and collect all intellectual material available. The best representatives of every field attended the Commonplace Books.

## Japan

The biggest Commonplace Book used to happen in Japan until about 500 years ago. Back then, in Japan, it still had its original taste and customs somewhat intact.
