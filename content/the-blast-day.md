# The Blast Day

This is the day when everyone who wanted to be saved was saved — safely transported to Mindland's territory.

Those who did not want to go were left behind.

Today's Earthers — those who were saved — watched the only home they had ever known blast apart. With their own eyes.

To them, it was a huge ball of fire in the sky, somewhere far away.

But.

But.
