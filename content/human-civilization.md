# Human Civilization

Human civilization is the starting context of the story. At the opening, it is in a very advanced stage.

## Origins

Humans initially lived on Earth. Life on Earth started coincidentally — through an autopoietic loop — and over vast time evolved into humans.

At some point, humans became capable of inventing tools and bending the material of the world for their own use. This triggered a second wave of intelligent evolution.

## Present State

Humans now populate both **Earth** and **Mars**. The **Moon** serves as:

- A fueling station
- A site for mining operations
- A location for experiments too dangerous to conduct on Earth or Mars

## Known Details

- [[characters/Ethal|Ethal]] is a scientist from Earth.
- [[characters/Prutha|Prutha]] is a scientist from Earth.
- The opening civilization feels scientifically advanced, established, and capable of producing major discoveries.
