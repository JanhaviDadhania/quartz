# Ethal

Ethal is a scientist from Earth and a central character in the story.

## Personality and Traits

Ethal is smart and academically ambitious. He is small in stature and bears a resemblance to Gödel.

His exact personality is not yet fully defined.

> **Writer's note:** Ethal and Wise Man both risk sounding like ancient Greek philosophers. That can't be right. The thoughts may recur across time — that genuinely happens — but the *expressions* must be different. How would a Gen Z person say the same idea versus how a Greek man would say it? Same thought, different voice. Keep that distinction alive on the page.
