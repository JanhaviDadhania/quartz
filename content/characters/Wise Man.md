# Wise Man

Wise Man is a character from [[mindland|Mindland]].

## The Challenge

Wise Man tells Ethal: if he can find the answer to this question, he can come and join the fancy science lab.

> **What is the use of politics in the perfect society?**

Ethal comes back not with an answer but with a statement:

> Greatest of all gifts is the power to estimate things of their true worth.

## From Wise Man's Books

> What is the role of politics in a perfect society? We write down positives and negatives, perform data analysis, predict probability, and take the next action. That is the mechanism to govern a world. Politics exists today because the probabilities carry high inefficiencies and our theories and predictive power are weak.

## On Art

> The noblest art appeals to the intellect as well as to the feelings. And this intellectual pleasure is the highest form of [?] to which a man can rise.

The function of art is **catharsis** — purification. If you are doing catharsis in war, it is harmful. If you are doing catharsis in theatre, it is harmless.

## Role in the Story

Wise Man serves as a gatekeeper and intellectual challenger, drawing Ethal toward Mindland's higher order of knowledge.
