# Prutha

Prutha is a scientist from Earth.

## On Living Fully

> If you draw, you are living your life more interestingly.
> If you sing, that's even better.
> If you speak, tell stories — you see things others are just blind to.
> If you train your muscles, that's better.

## The Realisation

Just a few years ago, Prutha had a realisation: she could be good at everything and anything she puts her time into. She now needs to decide what is important enough to put time into.

That question stayed the same even after Mindland saved Earthers. *What do I invest in?*
