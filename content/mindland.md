# Mindland

Mindland is a main setting and civilization in the story. It is vastly ahead of Earth — not in opposition to it, just operating on a different order of knowledge.

## Mindland and Matterland

Mindland did not disagree with Matterland. They were simply operating on the power set of knowledge — a level above.

## Relationship to Earth

Saving Earth was just one tiny task Mindland did, mainly as a charity. Mindland was in a race with other advanced civilizations and had no time to care deeply or be friends with Earth.

Mindlanders didn't hate Earthers. They were just a little too busy. And Earth was so far behind technologically that there was nothing Earthers could offer to help — or at least, that is how it seems at present, even to Ethal and Prutha.

They are hopeful, though.

## The Race

While Mindland races with other advanced civilizations, Earthers like [[characters/Ethal|Ethal]] and [[characters/Prutha|Prutha]] race to reach the level of Mindlanders.

Ethal and Prutha are not blind. They are just being practical. They know they have to take the first step to reach where they want to be. There is no shame in that. You put all you have into that step, right now.
